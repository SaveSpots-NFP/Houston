## Timing

NCUR is the only currently verified submission window. Its December 4, 2026 deadline is close; the working abstract should be drafted now and reviewed by the mentor/campus coordinator. SOPHE and APHA are monitoring targets rather than confirmed 2027 calls. Recheck these pages in November-January, the usual spring-symposium call window identified by the cohort guide, and update this table when official instructions appear.

## Poster production decision

Do not lock a poster canvas yet. The guide's supplied SaveSpots layout is 48×36 inches landscape, while NCUR 2027 allows up to 48×48 inches and APHA's current board guidance is materially different. Choose a format only after selecting a venue and verifying its exact maximum dimensions, orientation, mounting method, and whether the stated measurement refers to the board or the poster itself.
